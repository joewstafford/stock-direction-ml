## Data

**Instrument:** SPDR S&amp;P 500 ETF (SPY)#

Chosen for its high liquidity and data quality. As SPY is the most heavily traded ETF in the world, 
meaninbg tight spreads, minimal microstructure noise, and the deepest, cleanest
historical data available. This keeps data-quality risk close to zero.

**Period** 2016-09-01 to 2026-09-01 (10 years of daily bars adjusted close)

10 years spans the 2017 low-volatility bull run, the Q4 2018 selloff, the 2020 COVID crash and recovery,
the 2022 rate-hike bear market, and the 2023–2025 recovery. This matters
for testing whether any signal found generalizes across conditions rather
than being an artifact of one calm period. It also splits cleanly into five
2-year walk-forward validation blocks.
